Privacy

For the Homework Launchpad website and its apps (Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, iPhone and iPad). Last updated 29 October 2026.

The short version: we keep only what the Launchpad needs to work - your sign-in, your homework list, your progress and what you do in the games and shop. There are no adverts, no tracking and no selling of anything to anyone. Nothing here costs real money.

What we keep

  • Your sign-in. You sign in with a Google or Microsoft account. We keep the email address and the name that account gives us, so we know it's you.
  • Your homework planner. The things you add to your list, and whether they're done.
  • Learning progress. Which answers you got right in the learning games, so they can pick what to practise next.
  • Games and the shop. Scores, play time, the pence and Launchpad Pounds you earn and spend, the items you own, trades, savings and shares. All of this is pretend money.
  • Chat, the board and rooms. Messages you post where others can see them.
  • Your picture. The avatar you make. If you upload a photo, it is shrunk, its hidden location data is removed, and it only appears to others once the Launchpad's owner has approved it.

What we don't do

  • No adverts and no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We never sell or share your information with other companies.
  • Other people see your first name and initial, your picture and what you post - never your full email address. (If two people share a name, the part before the @ is added so you can tell them apart.)

Where it's kept

On the Launchpad's own server in London (UK), run on Oracle Cloud. Connections to it are encrypted (HTTPS). Your sign-in is also remembered on your own device, so you don't have to sign in every time.

Who can see it

You, and the Launchpad's owner and helpers who look after the site (for example to remove an unkind message or approve a photo). Google or Microsoft handle the sign-in step under their own privacy policies.

Keeping and deleting

Your information is kept while you use the Launchpad. You can delete things on your homework list yourself at any time. To have your whole account and everything in it deleted, ask the Launchpad's owner and it will be removed.

The apps

The apps show the same Launchpad as the website, so the same applies. The Windows download checks the website for updates; it sends nothing about you when it does. The camera and photos are only used if you choose to upload a picture.

Questions

Ask the Launchpad's owner, or a parent or teacher can get in touch on your behalf.
