Privacy

For the Homework Launchpad website, its other sites (like Level Up, the Suite and Launchpad AI) and its apps. Last updated 12 November 2026. Also read the Rules.

The short version: we keep only what the Launchpad needs to work. That's your sign-in, your homework list, your progress, what you make, and what you post. There are no adverts, no tracking and nothing is sold to anyone. Nothing here costs real money.

When you use Launchpad AI or the Game Maker, your message goes to an AI company to be answered. We take out personal details we can spot first, but never type personal information into an AI.

What we keep

  • Your sign-in. You sign in with Microsoft, Google or a password. We keep your email address and the name your account gives us, so we know it's you.
  • Your homework planner, to-dos and flash cards. What you add, and whether it's done.
  • Learning progress. Which answers you got right in the learning games, so they can pick what to practise next.
  • Games and the shop. Scores, play time, and the pence and Launchpad Pounds you earn and spend (all pretend money), plus the things you own and trade.
  • Things you make. Files in the Suite, games you make in the Game Maker, videos you post, your avatar and any photo you upload. Photos have their hidden location data removed and only appear to others once the owner approves them.
  • Chats and posts. Messages you post where others can see them, like the year chat and the board.
  • Reports. If you report something, we keep what was reported, the reason and any note you add. The person you reported is never told who reported them.
  • Launchpad AI chats. Your conversations, so you can come back to them. You can delete any chat. If a message is blocked by the safety check, we note that it happened.
  • AI memory (only if you turn it on). A few short notes, like the subjects you're revising. It never keeps personal details. You can see and delete every note, and turning memory off deletes them all.
  • Quick sign-in. When a device asks to sign in with a code, we keep the code, the kind of device ("Chrome on Windows") and a scrambled version of its internet address (to stop people trying lots of codes). They're deleted after an hour.

AI on the Launchpad

  • Launchpad AI, the explainer and the Game Maker send what you type to an AI company to get an answer: Groq, Cohere, OpenRouter, Cloudflare, NVIDIA, Google, SambaNova or Anthropic (Claude). Which one depends on the AI you pick, and which one is free to answer at the time.
  • Before anything is sent, the Launchpad takes out things it can spot, like email addresses, phone numbers, postcodes and your name. It can't catch everything, so don't type them.
  • Some free AI services may use what's sent to them to improve their AI. Claude (paid for with pence) does not.
  • A safety check reads messages to stop harmful requests. If someone seems to be in danger, the AI points them to help, such as Childline.

What we don't do

  • No adverts, and no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We never sell your information or give it to other companies for their own use.
  • Other people see your first name and initial, your picture and what you post. They never see your full email address. (If two people share a name, the part before the @ is added so you can tell them apart.)

Where it's kept

On the Launchpad's own server in London (UK), on Oracle Cloud. Connections to it are encrypted (HTTPS). Your sign-in and settings (like dark mode or the Notebook look) are also remembered on your own device, so you don't have to sign in every time.

Who can see it

You, and the Launchpad's owner and helpers who look after the site, for example to remove an unkind message or approve a photo. Microsoft and Google handle their sign-in step under their own privacy policies.

Keeping and deleting

Your information is kept while you use the Launchpad. You can delete your own to-dos, AI chats and AI memory yourself at any time. To have your whole account and everything in it deleted, ask the Launchpad's owner and it will be removed.

The apps

The apps show the same Launchpad as the website, so the same applies. The Windows download checks the website for updates, and sends nothing about you when it does. The camera and photos are only used if you choose to upload a picture or scan a quick sign-in code.

If you're under 13

Check with a parent or carer that it's OK for you to use the Launchpad. They can ask questions or ask for your account to be deleted on your behalf.

Changes and questions

If this page changes in a way that matters, you'll be asked to read it and agree again when you next sign in. Questions? Ask the Launchpad's owner, or a parent or teacher can get in touch for you.
