Rules

The terms of use for the Homework Launchpad, its other sites and its apps, in plain words. Last updated 12 November 2026. Also read Privacy.

The short version: be kind, keep personal things private, look after your account, and remember AI can get things wrong. Break the rules and you might lose some features or your account.

1. What the Launchpad is

A free homework site for pupils, made by HWL Systems: revision links, a planner, learning games, the Suite, Launchpad AI and more. Nothing here costs real money. Pence and Launchpad Pounds are pretend and can't be swapped for real money.

2. Be kind

  • No bullying, mean comments, swearing at people, or anything hateful, rude or scary.
  • Don't post anything you wouldn't want a teacher to see.
  • If you see something wrong, report it. Reports are private.

3. Keep personal things private

  • Don't share your address, phone number, passwords or where you'll be, and don't share anyone else's.
  • Don't post photos of other people without asking them first.

4. Look after your account

  • Only use your own account, and don't let anyone else use it.
  • Never give anyone your password.
  • Quick sign-in: only approve a device that is in front of you. Never type in a code someone sent you or asked you for, because that would give them your account.
  • Sign out on shared computers.

5. Using AI

  • AI can make mistakes. Check anything important.
  • Follow your school's rules on using AI for homework. If your teacher says to do it yourself, do it yourself.
  • Don't try to trick the AI into saying harmful things. A safety check reads messages, and trying to get round it can lead to a cooldown or AI being switched off for you.
  • Don't type personal information into the AI.

6. Things you make

What you make (games, files, videos, pictures) is yours. By posting it where others can see it, you let the Launchpad show it to them. Don't post things that aren't yours to share. The owner can remove anything that breaks these rules.

7. Don't break things

Don't try to hack, overload or get round the Launchpad's limits, use bots, or cheat in the games or the shop. If you find a bug, tell the owner instead. That's helpful and you won't get in trouble for it.

8. If the rules are broken

Depending on what happened, the owner or a helper can remove posts, switch features off for an account, reset pretend money or items, or pause an account. For something serious, a teacher or parent may be told.

9. The small print, made simple

  • The Launchpad is run as a school project. It might sometimes be down, closed for maintenance, or lose things. Keep your own copy of anything important, like downloading your Suite files.
  • Features can change, and some may be switched off or removed.
  • Links to other websites (like Sparx or BBC Bitesize) go to sites with their own rules.
  • If you're under 13, check with a parent or carer that it's OK for you to use the Launchpad.

10. Changes

If these rules change in a way that matters, you'll be asked to read them and agree again when you next sign in. Questions? Ask the Launchpad's owner.
