Everything from the last 30 days that wants a person rather than a filter — built fresh each time out of what already happened, so there is nothing here the chat log doesn’t already hold. Anything in red goes to your form tutor or the safeguarding lead the same day; it isn’t yours to handle on your own. Ticking one off only hides it from this list.

People

Turn somebody’s explainer or chat off while they’re messing about, and back on when they’ve stopped. The switches above do the same for everyone at once. Nothing here shows what anybody actually typed.

Look-only accounts

These can open this panel and run the checks, and nothing else — not the chat or board logs, not deleting, not closing the site. For showing the panel on somebody else’s laptop without signing in as yourself. Passwords aren’t stored anywhere, so to get one again you make a new one.

Asked for a room

Nothing exists until you say yes. Turning one down leaves no room and no members behind — only a note to whoever asked.

Private rooms

Only you can make a room or put somebody in one, so nobody can start a private chat with another pupil. Everything said in a room goes through the same filter, the same checker and the same pause as the year chat, and anything turned away lands in the same log. Each room says on its header that you can see it.

The site itself

Checking…

Closing it shows everyone a maintenance notice. You and your school account can still sign in. Remember to open it again — a closed site on Wednesday morning would be a bad surprise.

Games

Who can play the games (Tycoon, Dragons, Battle Stories, the Arcade, Gate Run and the doodles). New accounts start with games off until you switch them on. Three strikes also takes games away until midnight. Learning — Level Up and the dashboard — is never switched off here.

Switches

These apply to everyone at once, above the per-person ones. Yours keep working either way, so you can check before opening it back up.

Accounts

Add an account

Any address works here — school, Hotmail, Gmail. No confirmation email is sent. The explainer and the chat start switched off.

Shortcuts everyone gets

These appear on everybody’s dashboard under “From school”. Each person can put one away if they don’t use it.

Exceptions left open

These addresses are allowed in but no account was made. That means a create didn’t finish, and the way in is still standing open. Clear them.
