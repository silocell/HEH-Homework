What is this site?

A homework site built by a Year 7 pupil at Hatch End High School, for pupils there: one page with the school's revision sites, a word of the day, a to-do list, a dictionary look-up and a maths-and-English game. Accounts are only made for pupils at the school and sign in with their school Microsoft login.

No adverts, nothing to buy, no tracking. The code is public under an open licence at github.com/silocell/HEH-Homework, and questions are welcome through the school.
